## Daily and scheduled loop

Dailies are for materials, not gold; raids are the gold.

- **Kurzan Front** on all four T4 characters (1640+). Fate Embers only carry gold rewards at 1640 and above, so all four qualify. Treat Ember gold as a bonus, never as income.
- **Guardian raid** daily on all four. Check accessory and bracelet drops before dismantling; good rolls sell for real tradable gold.
- **Chaos Gates are scheduled, not daily.** Check the in-game calendar and run them on your highest characters when they're up. They replaced the old weekly Una's token gold.
- **Adventure Islands** on gold days: 2,373 to 3,630 gold at 1700 to 1719.
- **Selling materials:** sell unbound Destiny Leapstones and Destruction Stones only when you're not about to use them. Event milestones hand out the same materials free, so check the next milestone before buying from the market.

### Keep, sell or dismantle

Before dismantling anything good-looking, check its Auction House price (your pet gives remote access). If it sells, sell it.

| Drop | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Accessory below 67 quality | Dismantle |
| Ancient accessory, 67+ quality | Check the Auction House; equip it if it beats what you wear, otherwise sell |
| Relic accessory, 67+ quality | Send to Vismunde or Miriyanah for Enlightenment points; sell if neither needs it |
| Accessory with support (ally buff) effects | Keep the good ones for Vedinah; sell the rest |
| Ancient bracelet | Check the Auction House before dismantling; don't start rerolling one you might sell (rerolling binds it) |
| Uncut Relic or Ancient ability stone | Check the Auction House before faceting; a faceted stone usually can't be sold |
| Everything else | Dismantle |

Accessories can only be traded 3 times, so a piece that has already changed hands may not resell.

## Upgrade path

Your advanced-honing-to-30 plan still lands the main at 1710.8, and 1710 is now the breakpoint that matters most: Final Day Solo opens and both events pay their 1705 and 1710 milestones. Past 1710, solo gold stops growing (Act 4 Hard and Horizon Cathedral Stage 2 are group content), so 1720 is about the last event milestones.

1. **Finish advanced honing to 30 on every piece (1704 to 1710.8).** Remaining: helmet, chest and shoulders 20 to 30 (+30 piece levels), pants 23 to 30 (+7), weapon 26 to 30 (+4). Order doesn't change where you land, so do whichever piece is cheapest per level in the honing window.
   1. Solo gold at 1710: Final Day (32,000) + Act 4 (27,000) + Act 3 (21,000) = 80,000 a week, up from 64,500. Both events pay out at 1705 and again at 1710.
2. **Normal hone all six pieces to +20 (1710.8 to 1720).** That's five armor pieces +18 to +20 and the weapon +19 to +20, each normal level worth 5 item levels on its piece.
   1. Solo gold doesn't change at 1720. The reasons to go are the 1715 and 1720 event milestones, plus Act 4 Hard and Horizon Cathedral 2 if you group up later.
3. **Then accessories and Ark Grid.** Your event accessories are fine for now. Item level gates gold, so it comes first.

### Funding it

- Spend roster-bound gold first; save tradable gold for market buys. The alts' raids are mostly half roster-bound, so that half flows straight into the main's honing.
- Use both events' milestone materials before buying anything from the market.
- Alts hone only with their own character-bound materials; see Alt upgrade paths below. Don't spend gold or roster-bound materials on them until the main hits 1710.

### Special honing leapstones

- A special honing attempt is a free extra roll. A failure costs only the leapstones and doesn't add Artisan's Energy.
- **Use them on armor at +18.** Armor is 3% for 20 stones; the weapon is 1.5% for 50. That's 0.15% per stone on armor against 0.03% on the weapon, so armor is 5 times more efficient. Both give the same +5 item levels on success.
- Expected cost per success: about 667 stones on armor, about 3,333 on the weapon.
- Leave the weapon's +19 to +20 to normal honing.
- Skip any armor piece that already has a lot of Artisan's Energy built up. A success resets that progress, so let normal honing finish those.

## Alt upgrade paths (Vismunde and Miriyanah)

Both alts climb slowly on their own drops. Each solo Act they unlock is a bigger weekly jump than anything else they can buy, so hone toward the next Act's item level and ignore everything else.

| Target | Unlocks | Solo gold a week at that level | Honing needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1660 | Aegir Solo | 17,600 plus a third raid | Miriyanah: about +4 on every piece from +10 |
| 1670 | Act 2 Solo | 34,100 (Act 2, Aegir, Echidna) | About +2 per piece from 1660 |
| 1680 | Act 3 Solo | 49,000 (Act 3, Act 2, Aegir) | About +2 more per piece |
| 1700 | Act 4 Solo | 64,500 (Act 4, Act 3, Act 2) | About +4 more per piece |

Every 10 item levels is 12 normal honing successes spread across the gear (60 piece item levels). Five advanced honing levels count the same as one normal level.

**Order:** Vismunde to 1670 first; that alone doubles its gold (17,600 to 34,100). Miriyanah to 1660 next, for Aegir Solo.

### How each alt spends its materials

1. **Sort materials by binding.** Character-bound materials stay on the alt that got them. Roster-bound materials go to the main until 1710; check each tooltip.
2. **Normal honing is the engine.** Each success is +5 item levels on that piece. Tap the cheapest piece first; armor taps use Guardian Stones, which are cheaper than the weapon's Destruction Stones.
3. **Keep the weapon within 1 to 2 levels of the armor.** Solo clears depend on damage, and the weapon carries it.
4. **Advanced honing 1 to 20 is the side track.** It's cheap and adds 1 item level per level. Clear Echidna once to unlock it, then use it when normal honing is waiting on stones.
5. **Special leapstones:** compare chance per leapstone between pieces, as on the main, and take the best rate.
6. **Never buy market materials for these two,** and skip View More chests on them.

At 1640+ both alts also qualify for Kurzan Front and Fate Ember gold, so run Kurzan Front and a guardian raid on them daily.

Thaemine Solo opens at 1610 and its gold is fully roster-bound; the September notes didn't list the amount, so check it in game.

### Cygnus and Vedinah (\~1400)

Quest Cygnus and Vedinah through South Kurzan ("Blazing Flames of Warr", 1600 set) and North Kurzan ("The Way Forward", 1640 set) as Miriyanah did. A 1640 alt adds at least Echidna Solo gold each week for no honing cost.
